# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the **model workshop** (data, evals, trainer)

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Submitting user chats, credentials, or scraped personal data
- Attempting to use this repo to change or attack helloblue.ai

## Enforcement

Report to support@helloblue.ai or GitHub maintainers. Helloblue Inc may remove
contributions or restrict participation.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
